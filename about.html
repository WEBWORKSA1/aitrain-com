About AiTrain

AiTrain exists to make working people genuinely good at using AI — safely, practically and without hype.

Most people were handed AI tools with no instructions. Some teams got huge gains; many got nothing, or got burned by a confident wrong answer. The difference is almost always skill: knowing what to ask, what to share, and what to check.

What we do

  • Free learning lines — six structured routes from fundamentals to building AI features.
  • Original guides — practical how-tos and playbooks for specific jobs.
  • Resources — a tested prompt library, an independent tools directory, a glossary and curated video lessons.
  • Team training — hands-on programmes for organisations, delivered by our team and vetted partner trainers.

Why a transit map?

Learning AI isn't one course; it's a network. You might start on the Foundations Line, change at Central to Prompt Craft, and ride Workflow Automation to the end. Every station is a skill you can use that day.

How we stay free

Advertising, labelled sponsorships, team-training programmes and supporter donations. Read our editorial standards and funding disclosure.

Team AI readiness check

Ten questions, about three minutes. See where your team stands and what to train first. No email needed to see your score.

1. How many people on your team use an AI assistant at least weekly for work?
2. Does your organisation have a written AI use policy?
3. Is there an approved AI tool people can use with internal information?
4. How do people learn to use AI today?
5. How often do people check AI output for facts and figures before sharing?
6. Can people name specific tasks AI makes faster for them?
7. Does the team share prompts or templates?
8. Is anything automated with AI steps (e.g. sorting enquiries, summaries)?
9. Do you measure time saved or quality from AI?
10. How confident is leadership about AI risks and opportunities?

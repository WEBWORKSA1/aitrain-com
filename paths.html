Learning lines

Six free routes through practical AI skills. Each station is a short module with a reading, a video or an exercise. Mark your progress and switch lines at Central whenever you like.

Hover or tab to a station to preview it.

Foundations Line

Beginner · 6–8 hours · 7 stations

Plain-language grounding in how modern AI works, what large language models are good at, where they fail, and how to use them safely on day one.

For: Anyone who uses a computer at work and wants to understand what AI can and cannot do before relying on it.

Board this line

Prompt Craft Line

Beginner → Intermediate · 8–10 hours · 7 stations

A practical method for writing prompts that work the first time: role, context, task, constraints and format, then iteration and reusable templates.

For: People who already use an AI assistant and want consistent, high-quality results instead of lucky ones.

Board this line

Workflow Automation Line

Intermediate · 10–12 hours · 7 stations

Find the tasks worth automating, connect AI to the apps you already use, and measure the hours you get back.

For: Operators, marketers, analysts and small-business owners who repeat the same digital tasks every week.

Board this line

Build with AI Line

Intermediate → Advanced · 14–18 hours · 7 stations

From neural-network intuition to calling model APIs, retrieval over your own documents, simple agents and evaluation.

For: Developers, technical founders and curious builders who want to ship AI features or small AI products.

Board this line

AI for Leaders Line

All levels · 5–6 hours · 6 stations

Strategy without hype: pick use cases, set policy, run a pilot, train people and measure return.

For: Managers, founders and department heads deciding where AI fits, what to buy and how to get a team to actually use it.

Board this line

Responsible AI Line

All levels · 4–5 hours · 5 stations

Bias, accuracy, transparency, copyright and security in practical terms, with checklists you can apply this week.

For: Everyone who publishes, decides or advises with AI help — especially in regulated or customer-facing work.

Board this line

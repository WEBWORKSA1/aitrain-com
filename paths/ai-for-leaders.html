AI for Leaders Line

Strategy without hype: pick use cases, set policy, run a pilot, train people and measure return.

All levels5–6 hours6 stationsFree

Who it's for: Managers, founders and department heads deciding where AI fits, what to buy and how to get a team to actually use it.

What you'll be able to do

  • Rank use cases by value and risk
  • Write a one-page AI use policy
  • Run a 30-day pilot with clear success metrics
  • Plan team training that changes behaviour

The route

  1. Station 1: Where AI pays off

    A value-versus-risk grid for sorting ideas into do now, pilot, and wait.

  2. Station 2: Build, buy or switch on

    Compare built-in features, off-the-shelf tools and custom builds on cost, speed and control.

  3. Station 3: A one-page AI policy

    What staff can use, what data stays out, who approves new tools, and how to report issues.

  4. Station 4: The 30-day pilot

    Pick a team, a baseline and three metrics; decide in advance what result means scale up.

  5. Station 5: Training that sticks

    Why one-off webinars fail and how short, role-specific practice changes daily habits.

  6. Station 6: Measuring return

    Hours saved, quality, cycle time and adoption — and how to present them to a board.

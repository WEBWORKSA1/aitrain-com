Build with AI Line

From neural-network intuition to calling model APIs, retrieval over your own documents, simple agents and evaluation.

Intermediate → Advanced14–18 hours7 stationsFree

Who it's for: Developers, technical founders and curious builders who want to ship AI features or small AI products.

What you'll be able to do

  • Explain gradient descent and attention at an intuitive level
  • Call a model API and handle errors and costs
  • Build retrieval-augmented generation over your own files
  • Write evaluations that catch regressions

The route

  1. Station 1: Neural networks, visually

    Neurons, weights and layers through a visual explanation before any code.

  2. Station 2: How models learn

    Loss, gradient descent and backpropagation as one connected story.

  3. Station 3: Transformers and attention

    Why attention let models handle long text, and what that means for prompts and context windows.

  4. Station 4: Calling a model API

    Requests, parameters, streaming, rate limits and keeping costs predictable.

  5. Station 5: Retrieval over your documents

    Chunking, embeddings and search so answers cite your own sources.

  6. Station 6: Tools and simple agents

    Let a model call functions, check its own work, and know when to hand back to a person.

  7. Station 7: Evaluating AI features

    Test sets, rubrics and regression checks so quality doesn't silently drift.

Read along

  • How large language models work: tokens, training and prediction · 10 min
  • Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) explained · 9 min
  • How to evaluate AI outputs: test sets, rubrics and regression checks · 8 min

Responsible AI Line

Bias, accuracy, transparency, copyright and security in practical terms, with checklists you can apply this week.

All levels4–5 hours5 stationsFree

Who it's for: Everyone who publishes, decides or advises with AI help — especially in regulated or customer-facing work.

What you'll be able to do

  • Recognise where bias enters an AI-assisted decision
  • Disclose AI use appropriately
  • Understand the basics of AI and copyright
  • Reduce prompt-injection and data-leak risk

The route

  1. Station 1: Bias in, bias out

    How training data and prompts shape outputs, with a simple fairness spot-check.

  2. Station 2: Accuracy and accountability

    Who is responsible when AI is wrong, and how to document human review.

  3. Station 3: Disclosure and trust

    When and how to tell customers, readers or students that AI helped.

  4. Station 4: Copyright basics

    What is generally understood about AI outputs and source material, and when to ask a lawyer.

  5. Station 5: Security hygiene

    Prompt injection, data leakage and safe handling of AI-connected tools.

How to complete this line

Work through one station per sitting. For each: read the linked guide, watch the video where one is listed, then apply the idea to one real task from your own work and note the result. When you have done all stations, try the matching brief in this month's prompt challenge.

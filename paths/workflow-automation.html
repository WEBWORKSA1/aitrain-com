Workflow Automation Line

Find the tasks worth automating, connect AI to the apps you already use, and measure the hours you get back.

Intermediate10–12 hours7 stationsFree

Who it's for: Operators, marketers, analysts and small-business owners who repeat the same digital tasks every week.

What you'll be able to do

  • Audit a week of work and rank tasks by automation value
  • Build a no-code automation that calls an AI step
  • Design human review points so automation stays safe
  • Measure time saved and report it

The route

  1. Station 1: The task audit

    A one-week log template and a scoring rule (frequency × minutes × error cost) to find your best candidates.

  2. Station 2: AI inside tools you already use

    Email, documents, spreadsheets and meeting notes: switch on what is already paid for before buying anything new.

  3. Station 3: No-code automation basics

    Triggers, actions and data mapping — the three ideas behind every automation platform.

  4. Station 4: Adding an AI step

    Classify, summarise, extract or draft inside an automation, with prompts written for machines rather than people.

  5. Station 5: Human in the loop

    Where to insert approvals, how to sample outputs, and when an automation should stop itself.

  6. Station 6: Spreadsheets with AI

    Formula help, cleaning messy data and turning a table into a written summary.

  7. Station 7: Measuring the win

    Before-and-after timing, error rates and a one-page impact report your manager will read.

Common questions

How much does team training cost?

It depends on group size, format and how much tailoring you need. Tell us your team size and goals and you'll get a fixed quote within two business days — no obligation.

Which AI tools do you train on?

Whichever your organisation uses or approves. The core skills transfer across major assistants; examples are built in your own tools.

Can you help with EU AI Act AI-literacy requirements?

Our foundation module covers the topics organisations commonly use to meet AI-literacy duties, and we provide completion records. We don't provide legal advice; pair the training with your own compliance review.

Do you train outside North America?

Yes. Live virtual sessions work anywhere; on-site training depends on location.

Who delivers the training?

AiTrain programmes are delivered by our team and vetted partner trainers with hands-on experience in the relevant role.
